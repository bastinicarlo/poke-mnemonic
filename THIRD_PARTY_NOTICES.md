## Italian word lists

`mnemonic-words.json` is derived from the `1000_parole_italiane_comuni.txt` and
`60000_parole_italiane.txt` lists in
[napolux/paroleitaliane](https://github.com/napolux/paroleitaliane).
The lists are filtered to Italian words whose encoded consonants exactly match
their Pokédex number. The source repository and these lists are distributed
